Frequently asked questions

What people usually ask.

Does it work for any type of coaching?

Yes. Your network can work in fitness, career or finance — each coach supports their clients with the tools for their niche.

Does each coach get their own login?

Yes. Each coach logs into their own panel and sees only their clients. You, as the owner, see the whole team.

Can I use my own brand?

Yes. Your logo, your colors and your subdomain replace Pathway in the portal your clients see (depending on the plan).

How do I switch plans as my team grows?

Just reach out and we'll move your network up to the plan you need without losing any of your coaches' or clients' data.

Ready to organize your team?

Pick your plan or reach out and we'll set up your account with your team.
