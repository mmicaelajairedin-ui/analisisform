Pathway vs Paperbell

By Micaela Jairedin· July 2026· 6 min read

Quick answer

Paperbell is a great tool for selling packages — but it only solves half the problem.

  • Paperbell shines at packages, contracts, scheduling and billing in a single flow, but it only manages the clients you already have — it doesn't bring you new ones.
  • Pathway does that and brings you new clients through its marketplace, adds AI-powered tools, and works across several niches (career, fitness, finance).

Choose Paperbell if your only focus is selling and billing coaching packages. Choose Pathway if you want to run your business and get new clients, with AI tools and a client-generating marketplace.

If you're evaluating Paperbell, you've already seen it's one of the simplest, most elegant platforms for coaches who sell packages. And it's true: it's very good at that. But before you decide, it's worth putting it side by side with Pathway — especially if getting new clients is part of your pain.

Where Paperbell genuinely wins

We won't tell you Pathway wins at everything — that wouldn't be credible or true. Paperbell is the better choice if:

  • Your focus is selling coaching packages: contracts, scheduling, payments and receipts in a very polished, frictionless flow.
  • You want something ultra simple with a single flat-price plan and unlimited clients.
  • You already have a steady stream of clients and just need to manage and bill them cleanly.

It's a clean platform, fast to set up and much loved by the solo coach who makes a living selling packages. On that front, it's hard to beat.

Where Pathway wins

Pathway is the better choice if:

  • On top of managing clients, you want to get new ones: Pathway includes a directory/marketplace that generates demand. Paperbell doesn't.
  • You want AI-powered tools (resume/CV builder, ATS optimization, LinkedIn analysis, diagnostic reports).
  • You coach in career, fitness or finance (multi-niche) and value simplicity.
  • You want a white-label client portal that carries your brand, not the software's.
  • You want fixed, predictable pricing with 0% commission on your own clients.
The difference that matters most: Paperbell helps you bill and manage the clients you already have. Pathway does that and also is built to bring you new clients — the real problem for most independent coaches.

Can you migrate from Paperbell to Pathway?

Yes. You can export your client data from Paperbell and load it into Pathway. Because onboarding is simple and the portal already carries your brand, the transition is quick for you and your clients. If you'd like, we'll help you with the migration.

Conclusion

Both are good — they're aimed at different coaches. Paperbell is the pick for the coach who wants to sell packages with the least possible friction. Pathway is for the coach who wants to run their business and get new clients, with AI tools and across several niches. If client acquisition is part of your problem, the scale tips clearly toward Pathway. You can try it with the free 14-day trial.
