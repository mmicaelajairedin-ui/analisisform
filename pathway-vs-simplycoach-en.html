Pathway vs Simply.Coach

By Micaela Jairedin· July 2026· 7 min read
← Back
Quick answer

Simply.Coach is a solid platform for teams — but it solves only half the problem.

  • Simply.Coach shines at goal tracking and managing coaching teams and organizations, but it only manages the clients you already have — it doesn't bring you new ones.
  • Pathway is the alternative that brings you clients through its marketplace, adds AI-powered tools, charges fixed low pricing with 0% commission on your own clients, and works across niches (career, fitness, finance).

Choose Simply.Coach if you run a coaching team/organization or do executive/enterprise coaching. Choose Pathway if you're an independent coach who wants to run your business and win new clients.

If you're evaluating Simply.Coach, you've already seen it's a platform built around structured tracking and coaching teams. It's a good tool. But before you decide, it's worth putting it side by side with Pathway — especially if you're independent and if getting clients is part of your pain.

Where Simply.Coach genuinely wins (honest)

We're not going to tell you Pathway wins at everything — that wouldn't be credible or true. Simply.Coach is the stronger choice if:

  • You do executive, business, or enterprise coaching and need a platform built for that world.
  • You run a coaching team or organization: you need to manage multiple coaches, roles, and clients under one account.
  • Your focus is structured goal tracking with forms, worksheets, and client metrics.

It's a robust, well-designed platform for the teams and enterprise coaching segment. If that's your case, it's a serious choice.

Where Pathway wins

Pathway is the better option if:

  • Beyond managing clients, you want to get clients: Pathway includes a directory/marketplace that generates demand. Simply.Coach doesn't.
  • You want AI-powered tools (resume/CV builder, ATS check, LinkedIn analysis, diagnostic reports).
  • You're independent (not a team) and value fixed pricing and simplicity.
  • You want 0% commission on your own clients — you keep every dollar you bring in yourself.
  • You coach in career, fitness, or finance and want one platform that adapts to your niche.
The difference that matters most: Simply.Coach helps you manage and track the clients you already have. Pathway does that and is built to bring you new clients — the real problem for most independent coaches.

Can you migrate from Simply.Coach to Pathway?

Yes. You can export your client data from Simply.Coach and load it into Pathway. Because onboarding is simple and the portal already carries your brand, the transition for you and your clients is quick. If you'd like, we'll help you with the migration.

Bottom line

Both are good — they aim at different coaches. Simply.Coach is the choice for coaching teams and organizations, and for executive/enterprise coaching. Pathway is the choice for the independent coach who wants to run their business and win new clients, with AI tools and across multiple niches. If you're independent and client acquisition is your bottleneck, the scale tips clearly toward Pathway. You can try it with the free 14-day trial.
