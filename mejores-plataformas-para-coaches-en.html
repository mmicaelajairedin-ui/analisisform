Quick answer

Almost every platform for coaches does the same thing: manage the clients you already have. The real difference comes down to one thing that hardly any of them solve:

  • Does it bring you new clients? Only Pathway includes a directory/marketplace that generates demand. Everyone else just manages.
  • Does it charge commission on the clients you already had? With Pathway it's 0% on your own clients — you only pay if it brings you a new one.

Below we compare Pathway, CoachAccountable, Paperbell, Simply.Coach, Practice and Delenta — with their honest strengths — and tell you which one fits your case.

If you're looking for a platform or software to run your coaching business, the list of options is long and confusing. Most of them are good tools — but they solve only part of the problem: they help you administer the clients you already landed, not land more.

In this comparison you'll see the most-used options, what each does well, and the question that puts everything in order: does the platform only manage you, or does it also help you grow?

How we compared them

We looked at 6 criteria that actually move the needle for an independent coach:

  • Brings you clients — is there a directory/marketplace that generates demand?
  • 0% commission on your own clients — do you keep 100% of the revenue from clients you brought yourself?
  • Management / CRM — clients, sessions, follow-up, notes.
  • AI tools — reports, documents, automation.
  • White-label client portal — your brand, not theirs.
  • Price and niche — how much it costs and what kind of coach it's for.

The platforms, one by one (honestly)

Pathway

Career, fitness and finance · from US$29/mo · 14 days free

The only one that, on top of managing the business, includes a directory/marketplace that brings the coach clients. It adds AI tools (CV/resume, ATS, LinkedIn, reports), a client portal with the coach's brand, Stripe payments and scheduling. The AI assists the coach — it doesn't replace them.

Pros: brings you clients, multi-niche, 0% commission on your own clients, works in English (and Spanish).
Keep in mind: it's young; the marketplace grows as it adds more coaches.

CoachAccountable

General coaching · from ~US$20/mo

One of the most mature and powerful for tracking and accountability (client goals, tasks, metrics). Ideal if your focus is structured, long-term support.

Pros: depth in tracking and automations.
Keep in mind: a learning curve, and it doesn't bring you clients.

Paperbell

General coaching · from ~US$40/mo

Very simple and tidy for the solo coach: scheduling + payments + contracts + packages in a clean experience. A good first platform if you're starting from scratch.

Pros: easy to use, all the basics well handled.
Keep in mind: no AI, no marketplace.

Simply.Coach

Coaching / executive · from ~US$15/mo

Geared toward coaches and firms with a more corporate/executive angle, with team and client management. One of the few with some multi-language support.

Pros: good for executive/business and teams.
Keep in mind: no AI career tools, no marketplace.

Practice

Coaching / solopreneurs · from ~US$25/mo

A very polished interface for managing clients, forms, scheduling and payments. Elegant and modern.

Pros: design and user experience.
Keep in mind: no marketplace or specific AI.

Delenta

General coaching · from ~US$19/mo

An all-in-one for coaches: CRM, scheduling, payments, booking pages and group sessions. Good value for the feature set.

Pros: complete and affordable.
Keep in mind: doesn't generate client demand.

The point almost none of them solve: getting clients. Everything on this list manages the clients you already have. Pathway is the only one that also includes a directory/marketplace built so new clients find you — the real pain for most independent coaches.

Which one to choose for your case?

  • You want to manage your practice and also get new clients: Pathway.
  • Your focus is structured accountability: CoachAccountable.
  • You want the simplest way to start (scheduling + payments + packages): Paperbell.
  • You do executive/business coaching or work with teams: Simply.Coach.
  • You care a lot about design and experience: Practice.
  • You want an affordable all-in-one: Delenta.
