FAQ

Frequently asked questions.

How does the 14-day trial work?

You have 14 days to try the whole product: set up your profile, add up to 5 clients and use the AI tools. At the end you can continue on the plan you choose or cancel with no penalties.

Does Pathway take a commission on what I charge my clients?

No. Pathway is a flat subscription. You charge your clients directly via your Calendly + your Stripe (or whatever you prefer). Pathway never touches that transaction or takes a percentage.

Do my clients see the Pathway brand or just mine?

The AI tools appear under your brand: your logo, your color, your link. Pathway works as invisible infrastructure. Your clients know you exist — not us.

Can I cancel anytime?

Yes, no penalties. You cancel from the dashboard and your account stays active until the end of the paid period. Your data stays exportable for 90 days.

Which AI tools are included?

ATS CV optimizer, job-offer analysis, interview simulator with feedback, question bank, search plan and more. All usable by your clients from their personalized dashboard under your brand.

Who is Pathway's ideal customer?

Career coaches, mentors, career-transition consultants or independent HR pros. Professionals who already charge for their time and want to multiply their impact without building the tech infrastructure.

Shall we start?

14-day free trial to test everything. Your public profile live in 5 minutes.
