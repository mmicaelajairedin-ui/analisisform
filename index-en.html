FAQ

Frequently asked questions.

How does the 14-day trial work?

You get 14 days to try the whole product: set up your profile, add up to 5 clients and use the AI tools. At the end of the trial you can continue with the plan you choose or cancel with no penalty.

Does Pathway charge commission on what I charge my clients?

No. Pathway is a flat subscription. You charge your clients directly via your Calendly + your Stripe (or whatever method you prefer). Pathway doesn't touch that transaction or take a percentage.

Do my clients see the Pathway brand or only mine?

The AI tools appear under your brand: your logo, your color, your link. Pathway works as invisible infrastructure. Your clients know that you exist — not us.

Can I cancel anytime?

Yes, with no penalty. You cancel from the dashboard and your account stays active until the end of the period already paid. Your data stays exportable for 90 days.

Which AI tools are included?

ATS CV optimizer, job-offer analysis, interview simulator with feedback, question bank, job-search plan and more. All usable by your clients from their personalized dashboard under your brand.

Who is Pathway's ideal client?

Career coaches, mentors, career-transition consultants or independent HR professionals. People who already charge for their time and want to multiply their impact without building the technical infrastructure.
