Quick answer

Practice is a beautifully designed tool — but it only solves half the problem.

  • Practice shines at user experience and clean client management for solopreneurs, but it only manages the clients you already have — it doesn't bring you new ones.
  • Pathway does that and brings you clients with its marketplace, adds AI-powered tools, and works across niches (career, fitness, finance).

Choose Practice if you want the most polished, simple client management. Choose Pathway if you want to run your business and get found by new clients.

If you're evaluating Practice (practice.do), you've already seen it's one of the best-designed platforms for coaches and solopreneurs. It's a great tool. But before you decide, it's worth putting it side by side with Pathway — especially if getting clients is part of what keeps you up at night.

Where Practice genuinely wins (honest)

We won't tell you Pathway wins on everything — that wouldn't be credible or true. Practice is the better choice if:

  • You value, above all, a flawless user experience: a clean, fast, pleasant interface for you and your client.
  • You're a solopreneur who wants tidy client management, with a free plan to get started.
  • Beautiful design and polish are what you're optimizing for, and you already have a steady flow of clients coming in.

It's a modern, carefully crafted platform that's genuinely loved for its design. For the coach who prioritizes experience, it's a great pick.

Where Pathway wins

Pathway is the better choice if:

  • Beyond managing clients, you want to get clients: Pathway includes a directory/marketplace that generates demand. Practice doesn't.
  • You want AI-powered tools (resume, ATS check, LinkedIn analysis, diagnostic reports).
  • You coach in career, fitness, or finance (multi-niche) and want everything integrated (scheduling included, no separate Calendly).
  • You want fixed, predictable pricing with 0% commission on your own clients.
  • You want a client portal with your own brand (white-label).
The difference that matters most: Practice helps you neatly manage the clients you already have. Pathway does that and is built to bring you new clients — the real problem for most independent coaches.

Can you migrate from Practice to Pathway?

Yes. You can export your client data from Practice and load it into Pathway. Because onboarding is simple and the portal already carries your brand, the transition for you and your clients is quick. If you'd like, we'll help you with the migration.

Bottom line

Both are good — they aim at different coaches. Practice is the pick for the coach who prioritizes a flawless user experience for the clients they already have. Pathway is for the coach who wants to run their business and get found by new clients, with AI tools and across multiple niches. If bringing in clients is part of your problem, the scale tips clearly toward Pathway. You can try it with the 14-day free trial.
